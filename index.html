Divya Darshan (DD) guidance

TTD's published FAQ says DD tokens are available round-the-clock at Galigorpuram footpath and from 05:00 AM to 06:00 PM at Srivari Mettu footpath. This is published guidance, not a live token counter. Confirm current arrangements before starting the footpath.

Accommodation

Useful guidance for rooms, rest houses and lockers at Tirumala.

TTD does not publish public live room-vacancy counts in the sources connected to this app. We can automatically refresh published room rules and announcements, but current vacancy must be checked at TTD counters or its official booking portal.
